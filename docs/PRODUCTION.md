# Google Vids, Substack, and YouTube handoff

Production starts only after human review and successful packaging. Account access
and feature availability in Google Vids still need to be confirmed by the student;
this project does not purchase or enable a subscription.

For each piece, use `package/01-article.md`, `01-video_script.md`, and `01-sources.md`
(substitute 02 and 03). The script includes scene directions, narration, and source
references. Create a Google Vids project, build the scenes, record or generate
narration using features available to your account, and review the resulting
3–5 minute video. An avatar is optional. Use readable source cards and preserve
uncertainty in the spoken argument. Identify AI-generated presentation where used.
Production notes and internal review status are not narration or audience-facing
copy. Resolve their checks, remove draft-only notes, and record any factual edits
before final approval; content edits require a fresh review fingerprint.

Publish the corresponding 600–900 word Substack article with shareable access and
no paid subscription requirement. Upload the video to YouTube as public or unlisted.
Link the video from the article; include the article and understandable evidence
references in the video description. Replace all placeholder URLs with real links.

Fill each record in `package/publication_links.json` with the real Substack and
YouTube URLs. Open every URL while signed out to verify course staff can access it.
Copy those links into `SUBMISSION.md`.

The separate 4–6 minute narrated technical demonstration is not one of the three
audience videos. Its outline is in [DEMONSTRATION.md](DEMONSTRATION.md).
