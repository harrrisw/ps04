# Source selection and limitations

The current topic is AI-assisted customer, competitor, and market research for
small businesses. Configured sources are discovery routes, not automatic endorsements.
The editorial stage examines substantive evidence and can reject any candidate.

| Producer | Discovery | Perspective / incentive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marketing AI Institute | Blog RSS and its linked @aishowpod channel | Practitioner education; commercial training and events. Both endpoints count as one producer. |
| quantilope | Resource RSS | Research platform vendor; product and case-study claims need scrutiny. |
| Crayon | Blog RSS | Competitive intelligence vendor; enterprise results may not transfer to small businesses. |
| STRAT7 | Blog RSS | Research consultancy; practitioner knowledge and commercial incentives. |
| TechCrunch | AI-category RSS | Technology journalism; much coverage may be off-topic and rejected. |
| Insight Platforms | YouTube | Research practitioners and research technology discussion; assess sponsorship and relevance. |
| Greenbook | Podcast RSS | Industry interviews; guest and sponsor incentives, not controlled tests. |
| Christopher Penn / Trust Insights | Blog RSS | Applied AI methods, data quality, and audience research; consulting and training incentives. |
| Nielsen Norman Group | Article RSS | Research methods and observed AI workflows; assess study design and transfer to market research. |
| NewMR / The Future Place | A selected research-workflow YouTube webinar | Practical examples from Ray Poynter; training, consultancy, and sponsorship incentives. |

These are different originating organizations for provisional publisher counting.
This does not make repeated announcements independent evidence. Verify ownership,
guest affiliations, syndication, episode clips, and shared underlying studies in
human review. Known copies should share an `underlying_id` via documented overrides.
Identical extracted content and same-producer titles are also flagged mechanically.

Likely missing perspectives include independent small-business users, failed
implementations, non-English users, and customers whose data is processed. The
configuration is commercially weighted. Explain that limitation, and add rigorous
independent studies or practitioner material when eligible recent evidence exists.

Several initially probed feeds returned 404/403 or only old material and were not
included. The initial Marketing AI YouTube handle was inactive; the publisher's
own website linked to @aishowpod. The first run retains the failed collection
attempt; a fresh run uses the corrected source configuration.

On October 16, Parley rejected the two fresh general-AI podcast videos as unrelated
to a concrete market-research workflow. The pipeline correctly blocked briefing
because no accepted YouTube evidence remained. A subsequent run adds NewMR's
[September research-workflow webinar](https://newmr.org/play-again/5-ai-tips-september-2026/),
whose publisher page links directly to the configured YouTube recording. Its
upload/release date and transcript are collected normally, and selection is still
made against the same policy. The earlier rejected items and failed run remain
intact. This curated video URL must be refreshed when it ages outside the window;
it is not an evergreen guarantee of sufficient video coverage.
