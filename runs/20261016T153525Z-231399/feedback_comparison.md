## Arguments

- 01: The main change is that AI tools are moving from generic generation toward connected, source-linked workflows that can read internal files, pull context from approved apps, and return outputs tied back to source material. For small-business researchers, that matters because the bottleneck is no longer just producing summaries; it is finding the right evidence, keeping the chain of custody visible, and fitting the work into a narrow, manageable workflow.
  Previous: The useful development is access to research material inside AI workflows, illustrated by Crayon’s announced Insights API. Small businesses should assess that capability alongside newly documented maintenance and oversight constraints. Easier access merits a bounded pilot; it does not establish affordable, dependable autonomous research.

- 02: The strongest claims are not that AI replaces human analysis, but that it can help teams organize evidence, retrieve context, and flag discrepancies faster than manual review. The tension is that many attractive product claims are still vendor claims, while the more defensible guidance keeps returning to source traceability, narrow scope, and human checking. In short: AI can assist research workflows, but the current evidence does not justify treating these tools as autonomous validators.
  Previous: The evidence supports specific practices and attributed observations: supply classification context, preserve links to original research, and inspect agent-proposed corrections. It does not establish comparative accuracy, lower total cost, or reliable time savings. The central tension is between reducing manual work and retaining enough review to detect unsupported conclusions.

- 03: Small businesses should pilot AI where the task is bounded, source-linked, and reversible: finding context, classifying incoming material, surfacing discrepancies, and drafting a first pass for human review. They should not pilot it as an autonomous sender, deleter, or final arbiter of truth. The next thing to watch is whether vendors publish worked comparisons with named baselines and visible error handling rather than just more capability announcements.
  Previous: Start with one bounded question and retained research material. Require traceable findings, name the person responsible for interpretation, and record preparation, checking, and maintenance effort against the current manual process. Judge whether the pilot improves decision support before expanding automation.
