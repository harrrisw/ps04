The agency model is being rewritten in real time.
A growing gap is emerging between those embracing artificial intelligence and those sticking with the status quo.
The most forward-thinking agencies aren’t simply adding AI to existing services. They’re reimagining what an agency can be, developing AI-powered systems, expanding into consulting, and finding entirely new ways to deliver strategy, creative, technology, and operations.
Below, we explore several emerging agency models and services, along with how they could help your business move forward.

Meet the Agencies Redefining Marketing Services with AI
Algomarketing offers two service paths to People Transformation and/or Process Transformation. They do this by deploying senior practitioners within existing teams to study existing practices and workflows. From there, they co-build AI-native agents that can optimize and automate existing tasks. Beyond building AI enablers, Algomarketing offers coaching to help teams truly adapt and make the most of new tools at their disposal.
Demand Spring is a B2B revenue marketing consultancy that helps organizations connect marketing strategy, technology, and execution to drive measurable growth. Its services span marketing strategy, revenue growth, marketing technology consulting, and AI advisory, including ABM, buyer journey optimization, SEO and AEO, marketing automation, and MarTech implementation. They help marketing teams move beyond AI experimentation by developing strategies, automating workflows, training teams, and establishing the governance needed to operationalize AI at scale.
EMMIE Collective provides fractional Marketing Operations and Revenue Operations expertise, embedding experienced operators directly into client teams. Rather than functioning like a traditional agency, EMMIE provides flexible, on-demand support across platforms and functions including Marketo, HubSpot, Salesforce, Tableau, marketing automation, and data operations.
Fjorge is a digital development and technology partner that helps businesses build, launch, and maintain custom digital experiences. They offer website development, custom web applications, mobile apps, e-commerce, and ongoing technical support and maintenance. Rather than operating as a traditional marketing agency, Fjorge works behind the scenes as a development partner, often embedding with creative and marketing agencies to provide the technical expertise needed to bring complex digital ideas to life.
Intercept offers services including strategy, content and creative, digital media, sales enablement, and channel marketing, with a strong focus on enterprise tech and complex buying journeys. What sets Intercept apart is its AI-native delivery model: it uses proprietary AI tools and workflows to accelerate research, content production, personalization, campaign execution, and measurement while keeping strategy and creative human-led. Its Intercept Labs also works with clients to test and develop new AI-driven marketing approaches.
Level Agency uses AI orchestration to support brands with complex buying journeys and high-consideration purchases. Its services span traditional agency specialties, including digital marketing strategy, media planning, SEO and AI search, content creation, CRM and automation, and data science. The difference is in how those services are delivered. Level builds repeatable, AI-powered systems designed to connect strategy, execution, and data across the customer journey.
Seer Interactive has been in the digital marketing space for 20+ years, working with some of the world’s biggest brands. Today, the company continues to offer a broad range of digital marketing services while expanding its work in AI research, development, and strategy. Seer builds AI into its services through internal experimentation within its very own AI Lab. That hands-on experience has also shaped SeerAdvance, its custom AI consulting offering.
Soarion Digital is leaning into the importance of AI search, blending AI technology and human expertise to optimize client websites. They use a proven process to help clients become the go-to answer in AI search tools, starting with a new search foundation, building citation-driven content, earning third-party validation, and more. Through weekly updates, Soarion Digital grows AI referral traffic gradually.
