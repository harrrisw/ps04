I am now more convinced than ever that AI visibility softare is bullshit.
Inspired by Lily Ray, I grabbed all my very long query data out of Google Search Console via the API and started looking at it to see what kinds of AI chats were getting passed along as queries in GSC.
First, a lot of these synthetic queries are things no human is ever going to type in a regular chat. You’re going to get profoundly unrealistic results – you can see them in the screenshot. Here’s one:
i am a fan of football, soccer, boxing or mixed martial arts and i am open to grilling. i also enjoy adventure activities like camping, fishing, and hunting, and thus have a need to cook food outdoors. i’m looking for charcoal starter (chimney & lighting fluid) brands that are the best in terms of additives and chemicals used. list some brands, and tell me for each why would you, or wouldn’t you, recommend them; keep your rationale in the context of additives and chemicals used.
Now come on, no one talks like that, even to AI. We ask questions like “Why does my steak taste like gasoline?” and have similar conversations. So already, things are not going great. AI visibility software using synthetic queries like this isn’t going to tell you squat about how real people talk or what results will be returned.
