Every insight in Crayon, ready for whatever you build
The Insights API makes every insight Crayon collects for you available to your AI tools or your development team. It's the same data layer that runs Crayon. What you do with it is up to you.
You also get the full detail behind each insight, not just a headline. Every insight comes back with its title, summary, date and the competitors it's tagged to. Ask for more and you get the full text and source link, plus the specifics for that kind of insight: a review's rating, pros, cons and reviewer role, a call clip's date and quote, or a win/loss record's opportunity, amount, owner and the competitor you lost to.
The part that takes years is already done
Putting a summary on top of clean data takes a weekend. Getting that data clean takes years. Someone has to watch competitor websites, pull in your calls, read the reviews, capture win/loss, and then figure out what's new and what actually matters against a specific competitor. Then keep all of it running.
That's the work Crayon already does every day. We'd rather hand you the years and let you keep the weekend.
