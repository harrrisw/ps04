How to Make Better AI Tools: Why Data Quality Is the Lever You Control Today

October 8, 2026

·

Christopher S Penn

·

AI, Artificial Intelligence, Data, Generative AI, LinkedOut




View as Markdown

View converted Markdown


How do we make better AI tools?

This is the hottest topic right now in the field because model makers are saying they need to slow down the development of new models in the USA while it’s full steam ahead everywhere else in the world, particularly China.

When it comes to making AI smarter, there’s three levers that we can pull. The first is the model itself. Smarter models are generally better. And given a choice between a less smart model and a smarter model, at the same cost we should choose the smarter one. That’s pretty obvious.

The second lever we can pull is the harness. If the model is the engine, the harness is the rest of the car. A Ford Model T and a BYD U9 electric sports car are very different vehicles. If you could retrofit each vehicle with the same engine, you’d still get very different performance.

The third lever we can pull is the data. If we’re extending the car analogy, this is the fuel that you put into it. If you put in no fuel or you put in crappy fuel, even the best engine is going to choke.

Right now, techniques like recursive self-improvement (RSI) are focused on the model side of things, and we’re seeing impressive results from this in models like DeepSeek V4.1. We’re also seeing big changes in harnesses as more people explore alternative harnesses besides what the big tech companies provide.

But the lever that you have the most control over TODAY is what data you’re putting into AI. Even the smartest AI will have trouble making any sense of the tossed salad that is in your ERP or CRM system. And no AI model is smart enough, absent any other context, of making sense of 30 years of bad data governance.

Better results comes from better data. This has been true forever, and this is more true than ever in the age of AI.
