As the creator of MAICON, co-host of The Artificial Intelligence Show, and co-author of two books on artificial intelligence (Marketing Artificial Intelligence and The AI Transformatiion Blueprint), Roetzer has spent a decade helping professionals and leaders understand what AI actually means for the way work gets done.
Roles That Will Define the Future of Work
AI is reshaping work at every level of the organization. As intelligent agents take on more of the tasks once performed by humans, Roetzer argues that the future of business may belong to three emerging roles:
- The architect, who designs AI-native systems of work
- The orchestrator, who directs humans and machines toward outcomes
- The apprentice, who learns judgment in a world where traditional entry-level work is disappearing
This framework helps leaders make sense of a shift already underway and gives them a clearer way to think about how their organizations, teams, and career paths need to evolve. Roetzer has been discussing this framework on various platforms leading up to MAICON, including his ExecAI Insider newsletter and podcast, The Artificial Intelligence Show.
Judgment as the Most Valuable Skill
A key question Roetzer will tackle is what happens when AI compresses the traditional career ladder. His keynote explores why judgment becomes the most valuable skill in the organization and what leaders must do now to build smarter systems, stronger teams, and more resilient paths to expertise.
