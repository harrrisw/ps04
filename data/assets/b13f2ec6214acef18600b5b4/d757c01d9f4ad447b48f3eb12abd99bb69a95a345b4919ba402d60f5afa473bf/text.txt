It’s two weeks to one of the biggest events of the year, the annual Marketing AI Conference, MAICON, put on by SmarterX.
Which means today, right now, is the perfect day to get your COVID and flu boosters if you’re planning to attend.
Most vaccines take at least a week to ramp up, and you reach peak immunity in about two weeks (some variances because of age, health, etc. but two weeks is a good rule of thumb).
And when you check the State of Ohio’s disease wastewater tracking – the most reliable measure of prevalent disease because you can’t lie when you crap – you’ll see that while influenza and RSV are low, COVID is on the rise in Cleveland specifically. It’s the back to school special – as kids go back to school, they bring home more disease, and that in turn leads to the annual fall and winter surges.
I got my boosters this morning for both flu and COVID because I know for someone my age and health, two weeks is the sweet spot. I’ll still wear an N95 mask in the big gatherings (I use the very comfortable 3M Aura) because vaccines are the last line of defense, not the first, and no one enjoys conference crud.
But in a field where we’re concerned about cognitive offloading and deskilling, the last thing you need is a bout of COVID to further reduce your human capabilities.
COVID immunity from boosters wanes over 3-6 months (again depending on health) but this will carry me through the rest of conference season.
Get boosted, and see you in Cleveland or at other fine events this fall.
